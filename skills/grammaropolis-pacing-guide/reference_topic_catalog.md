# Topic catalog for pacing

Every Grammaropolis topic a pacing guide can schedule: the grades it's taught at, whether it's free, what it builds on, and how long it usually takes. Topic names here match the alignment maps character for character, so a topic can be looked up in a grade section by name.

**This file never assigns codes.** Whether a topic teaches a code at a grade, and which one, comes only from that grade's section of `reference_alignment_ccss.md` or `reference_alignment_teks.md`. A topic can be taught at a grade and still have no row in that grade's topic table; in that plan it's labeled "(enrichment)."

**Two-mode lookup.** If a Grammaropolis connector or MCP tool is available in the conversation, use it to confirm a topic's grades and free status; it's always current. This file is the fallback and ships with the skill so it works with no outside tools.

**Grades 1-8 only.** No topic below is taught in Kindergarten or high school.

---

## The topics

"Weeks" is the usual length at 45-119 minutes of grammar time per week (see `reference_pacing_rules.md` for other amounts). "Builds on" lists the topics a class should meet first, **counting only the ones taught at the plan's grade**: a Builds on topic that isn't taught at that grade is ignored (Sentence complements at Grades 7-8, for example, where Sentence objects isn't taught). A topic scheduled before one it builds on is a sequencing error unless the plan marks it as a deliberate preview.

### The Writing Company (four writing modes, plus craft moves)

| Topic | Grades taught | Free | Builds on | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Write a Story | 1-8 | No | Nouns (Nelson the Noun), Action verbs (Vinny the Action Verb), Adjectives (Jake the Adjective) | 2-3 |
| Write to Explain | 1-8 | No | Complete sentences | 2-3 |
| Write to Persuade | 1-8 | No | Conjunctions (Connie the Conjunction) | 2-3 |
| Write to Describe | 1-8 | Yes, every grade | Adjectives (Jake the Adjective), Nouns (Nelson the Noun) | 2-3 |
| Specific nouns (craft move: trading a vague noun for a precise one) | One page for every grade | Yes | Nouns (Nelson the Noun) | Part of a week |
| Strong verbs, show don't tell, sentence variety, formal and informal voice, three-part structure (craft moves) | One page each for every grade | No | The matching grammar topic | Part of a week |

Schedule writing modes only when the teacher's block covers writing, roughly one per grading period, each after the grammar topics it leans on. Craft moves never take a whole week; they ride inside a mode's weeks or a short week, and they carry no code of their own.

### Wonderful Words (vocabulary)

| Topic | Grades taught | Free | Builds on | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wonderful Words vocabulary units | 1-8 (units 1-6 at every grade; unit 7 at Grades 3-8; unit 8 at Grades 3, 4, 6, 7, and 8) | The first unit at every grade; the rest aren't | (none) | Their own column, one unit every 3-4 Instruction weeks |
| Smile or Frown (synonyms and antonyms) | One page for every grade | Yes | (none) | Part of a week |

Vocabulary units run beside the grammar topics in their own column rather than taking grammar weeks, and only when the teacher's block covers vocabulary. If a grade's unit count doesn't fit the Instruction weeks, schedule fewer units and say so.

**Free topics, in one place.** Nouns (Nelson the Noun), End marks, Subjects and predicates, and Write to Describe are free lessons at every grade, and so is the first Wonderful Words unit at each grade. Specific nouns and Smile or Frown are free pages for every grade. No other topic is free. If a Grammaropolis connector is available, it has the current free list and wins over this one. A plan marks free topics with "(free)" and explains the mark once in its legend.

## Default order by grade band

A starting sequence to adapt: drop topics not taught at the plan's grade, respect "Builds on," move code-bearing topics ahead of enrichment ones, and, only when weeks run short, cut enrichment first (see `reference_pacing_rules.md`).

**Grades 1-2.** Nouns (Nelson the Noun), Subjects and predicates, End marks with Sentence types, Action verbs (Vinny the Action Verb), Capitalization, Complete sentences, Adjectives (Jake the Adjective), Pronouns (Roger the Pronoun), Linking verbs (Lucy the Linking Verb), Adverbs (Benny the Adverb), Commas, Prepositions (Li'l Pete the Preposition), Conjunctions (Connie the Conjunction), Apostrophes (Grade 2), Interjections (Izzy the Interjection) with Interjections in a sentence (Grade 1), Interjections in a sentence (Grade 2).

**Grades 3-5.** Nouns (Nelson the Noun), Action verbs (Vinny the Action Verb), Subjects and predicates, Complete sentences, End marks (with Sentence types at Grade 3), Linking verbs (Lucy the Linking Verb), Adjectives (Jake the Adjective), Adverbs (Benny the Adverb), Pronouns (Roger the Pronoun), Capitalization, Prepositions (Li'l Pete the Preposition), Conjunctions (Connie the Conjunction), Commas, Clauses (Grade 5), Sentence structures, Sentence objects, Sentence combining, Apostrophes (Grades 3-4), Quotation marks, Sentence complements (Grades 4-5), Phrases and verbals (Grades 4-5), Semicolons and colons (Grade 5), Interjections (Izzy the Interjection) with Interjections in a sentence (Grade 3).

**Grades 6-8.** Nouns (Nelson the Noun), Subjects and predicates, Action verbs (Vinny the Action Verb), Linking verbs (Lucy the Linking Verb), Pronouns (Roger the Pronoun), Adjectives (Jake the Adjective), Adverbs (Benny the Adverb), Prepositions (Li'l Pete the Preposition), Phrases and verbals, Conjunctions (Connie the Conjunction), Clauses, Sentence structures, Complete sentences, Sentence combining, Sentence objects (Grade 6), Sentence complements, End marks, Commas, Semicolons and colons, Parentheses and brackets, Hyphens and dashes, Interjections (Izzy the Interjection) (Grades 6 and 8).

**Useful neighbors.** These teach better back to back: Conjunctions (Connie the Conjunction), then Commas (compound sentences), then Sentence structures; Prepositions (Li'l Pete the Preposition), then Phrases and verbals; Action verbs (Vinny the Action Verb), then Sentence objects; Linking verbs (Lucy the Linking Verb), then Sentence complements; Nouns (Nelson the Noun), then Apostrophes (possessives); Clauses, then Semicolons and colons.

## What no topic teaches

The in-scope list in each grade section says, standard by standard, which ones a topic teaches, which ones a topic only partly addresses ("Partly addressed in"), and which ones no Grammaropolis topic teaches; that list is the only source for any of the three. The usual untaught ones are handwriting, reference materials such as dictionaries and glossaries, and spelling (a few spelling standards are partly addressed, for example by the Grade 8 Pronouns lesson's confused-word pairs in Texas). Don't assume from this list: in the Common Core map the ellipsis is taught in the Grade 8 Hyphens and dashes lesson.
