# Grammaropolis for Teachers

Grammaropolis teaches grammar and writing through characters who are the parts of speech and the punctuation marks: Nelson the Noun files every name in town, Vinny the Action Verb is always mid-rescue, and the Mayor certifies good work. This plugin gives Claude six classroom tools built on that cast, for teachers of Grades 1 through 8, plus a read-only connection to the Grammaropolis lesson catalog.

## What's inside

- **Pacing guide:** plans your grammar year week by week from your calendar and standards, with a coverage check and a copy-ready version for Google Sheets.
- **Lesson planner:** builds one complete lesson on a topic or a standard, with a character-led hook and the full Learn, Assess, Practice, Create, and Certified cycle.
- **Daily sentence:** writes five-minute warm-ups, one day or a week at a time, with a key that explains every answer.
- **Exit tickets:** writes short checks and quizzes whose keys tell you what each wrong answer means, so you know what to reteach.
- **Prewriting toolkit:** runs one story, yours or ours, through eight prewriting techniques so students see how each one works.
- **Writing rubrics:** builds rubrics in Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Not Yet tiers for seven writing modes, with a student self-check.

## Use it

Ask Claude for what you need in plain words: "plan a lesson on commas for my 4th graders," "a week of daily sentences on pronouns," "an exit ticket on end marks," or "a rubric for a Grade 6 argument paragraph." Claude picks the right tool; if it reaches for the wrong one, name the one you want. Common Core codes are built in, Texas TEKS codes are looked up through the connector, and other states are matched to Common Core.

If you installed any of these skills from grammaropolis.com before, remove those copies, so Claude doesn't see two of each.

## Data

The skills run inside your own Claude conversation and never ask for students' names or work. The Grammaropolis connector at https://mcp.grammaropolis.com/api/mcp receives only catalog lookups, such as a topic, a grade, or a standards code. It needs no account or sign-in, it's read-only, and it stores nothing about you or your students. Privacy policy: https://mcp.grammaropolis.com/privacy
